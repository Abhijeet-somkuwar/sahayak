# Finding your way in Demo District Hospital

## Main gate and registration
Enter from the main gate on Station Road. The registration counters (Counter 1 and Counter 2) are straight ahead in the main hall on the ground floor. The token kiosk is to the left of Counter 1.

## Help desk
The help desk is at the entrance of the main hall, to the right of the main gate. Wheelchairs are available at the help desk.

## Ground floor
Room 1 (Medicine OPD) and Room 2 (Paediatrics OPD) are on the ground floor, in the corridor behind the registration counters. The Laboratory sample collection counter and the Pharmacy are at the end of the same corridor. The Casualty (Emergency) is in a separate building to the right of the main gate, with its own entrance and a red sign.

## First floor
Take the stairs or the lift next to the help desk. Room 3 (Women's OPD), Room 4 (Orthopaedics), Room 5 (Eye OPD) and Room 6 (ENT) are on the first floor. The plaster room is next to Room 4.

## X-ray and ultrasound
The X-ray room and ultrasound room are on the ground floor, left of the main hall, past the Pharmacy. Follow the yellow line on the floor.

## Toilets and drinking water
Toilets and drinking water are available on both floors near the stairs. A separate accessible toilet is on the ground floor near the help desk.

## Ayushman (PMJAY) desk
The Ayushman Mitra desk is on the ground floor next to Counter 2.
