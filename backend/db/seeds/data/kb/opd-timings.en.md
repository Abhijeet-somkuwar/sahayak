# Demo District Hospital — OPD timings

## General OPD registration
Registration counters open from 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. Registration is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Emergency (Casualty) is open 24 hours every day.

## Medicine OPD (General Medicine)
Medicine OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Room 1 on the ground floor. Diabetes and blood pressure follow-up patients are seen in the same OPD.

## Paediatrics OPD (Children)
Children's OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Room 2, ground floor. Immunisation (vaccination) is done on Wednesdays and Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 12:00 noon in the Immunisation Room next to Room 2.

## Gynaecology and Obstetrics OPD (Women)
Women's OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Room 3, first floor. Antenatal (pregnancy) check-ups are on all OPD days. Pregnant women are seen in the protected lane.

## Orthopaedics OPD (Bones and joints)
Orthopaedics OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Room 4, first floor. Plaster room is next to Room 4.

## Eye OPD (Ophthalmology)
Eye OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 12:30 PM in Room 5, first floor. Cataract screening camp is held on the first Monday of every month.

## ENT OPD (Ear, nose and throat)
ENT OPD runs from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM in Room 6, first floor.

## Evening OPD
A limited evening Medicine OPD runs from 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM on Monday, Wednesday and Friday in Room 1.

## Laboratory
Blood samples are collected from 8:00 AM to 11:00 AM at the Lab counter on the ground floor. Reports are collected from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM the same day, or the next working day for special tests. Bring your receipt to collect reports.

## Pharmacy
The hospital pharmacy (dispensary) is open from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. Medicines on the Essential Drug List are free of cost.
