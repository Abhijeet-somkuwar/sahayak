# Common cold and sore throat — home care advice (reviewed leaflet)

## What usually helps
Rest and drink plenty of warm fluids such as water, soup or warm water with honey (not for children under 1 year). Steam inhalation twice a day can ease a blocked nose. Gargle with warm salt water for a sore throat. Most colds get better within 5 to 7 days without medicines.

## When to come to the hospital immediately
Come to the hospital without delay if you have difficulty breathing, chest pain, a fever above 103°F (39.5°C) or fever lasting more than 3 days, severe throat pain with difficulty swallowing saliva, or if the patient is a child under 5 years, is pregnant, or has diabetes, heart, lung or kidney disease.

## What to avoid
Do not take antibiotics without a doctor's prescription. Do not give cough syrups to children under 4 years. Do not smoke and avoid smoke around the patient.
