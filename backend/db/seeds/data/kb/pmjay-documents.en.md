# Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (Ayushman card) at Demo District Hospital

## What is PM-JAY
Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY), also called the Ayushman Bharat scheme, gives eligible families cashless treatment of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for admission (in-patient) treatment at empanelled hospitals. This hospital is empanelled under PMJAY.

## Documents needed to make an Ayushman card
To create or verify an Ayushman card you need: Aadhaar card of the patient, ration card or the family ID shown in the PMJAY beneficiary list, and a mobile phone for OTP. Children can be added with their birth certificate and a parent's Aadhaar.

## Where to get the Ayushman card made
Go to the Ayushman Mitra / PMJAY help desk on the ground floor, next to the registration counters. The desk is open from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM on working days. Card creation is free of cost.

## Using the Ayushman card for admission
Show your Ayushman card (or the PMJAY ID) at the time of admission. The Ayushman Mitra will verify eligibility and raise the pre-authorisation. OPD consultation in this government hospital is already free for everyone; the card is needed for admission and surgeries.

## Checking eligibility
You can check eligibility by calling the toll-free number 14555 or by asking the Ayushman Mitra desk with your Aadhaar and ration card.
